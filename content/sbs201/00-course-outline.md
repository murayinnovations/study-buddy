## Learning goals

By the end of the course, a student should be able to:

1. Record transactions using double entry and extract a trial balance.
2. Identify and correct errors, including through a suspense account.
3. Calculate depreciation using the straight-line and reducing balance methods, and explain the choice of method.
4. Apply the accrual basis (matching) to accruals and prepayments.
5. Prepare a bank reconciliation statement.
6. Prepare an income statement, a statement of financial position and a statement of cash flows using the indirect method.

## Course conventions

Unless a question says otherwise:

- **Part-year depreciation** is charged on a monthly (pro-rata) basis.
- **Interest paid** is shown under operating activities in the statement of cash flows.
- **Dividends paid** are shown under financing activities.
- Amounts are in Kenya shillings (KES).
